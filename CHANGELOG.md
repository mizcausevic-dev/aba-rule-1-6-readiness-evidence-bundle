# Changelog

## [0.1] — 2026-05-30

### Added

- Initial profile.
- 8 obligation families covering ABA Rules 1.1c8 + 1.6 + 1.6(c) + 1.7 + 1.9 + 3.3 + 5.3 + 5.5, plus attorney-client privilege preservation, plus work-product doctrine preservation.
- 35 required evidence kinds total, each a discrete artifact (policy document, contract clause, attestation, or log).
- LegalTech-distinctive families: candor-toward-tribunal (anti-hallucination after Mata v. Avianca), and privilege-preservation as its own family (separate from confidentiality — privilege has waiver mechanics that confidentiality doesn't).
- Cross-reference to all 5 LegalTech 6-pack siblings + the cross-vertical Evidence Bundle spec/builder.

### Not yet

- Criminal-defense-specific overlay (Sixth Amendment effective-assistance + Brady).
- In-house-only overlay (in-house counsel have a subset of bar obligations).
- Foreign-jurisdiction overlay (Solicitors Regulation Authority for UK firms, Bar of Quebec, etc.).
- Per-state overlay datasets (state-bar opinions are referenced inside families, not separated).
